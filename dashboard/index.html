What are conformal intervals? Unlike Gaussian ±1.64σ bands, conformal intervals are distribution-free. They are calibrated on held-out residuals so the empirical coverage matches the stated level (~90%) regardless of the model's error distribution.

Method

How It Works

Split-conformal prediction adapted for time series

01

Data

Monthly NPCI UPI transaction data from Jan 2017 → Sep 2025 (104 observations). Log-transformed for variance stabilisation where needed.

02

Models

Seasonal Naive — carry forward the same month last year.
ETS — Holt-Winters multiplicative seasonality.
STL-ETS Hybrid — STL decomposition on log scale, ETS on seasonally-adjusted series.

03

Conformal Calibration

The training set is split: 75% fit, 25% calibrate. For each horizon h, we collect absolute residuals from rolling re-fits and take the ⌈(n+1)·0.90⌉-th smallest as the conformal width q̂ₕ.

04

Interval Construction

For a new forecast ŷ_{t+h}: interval = [ŷ_{t+h} − q̂ₕ, ŷ_{t+h} + q̂ₕ]. No distributional assumption. Guaranteed marginal coverage by the conformal guarantee.

05

Backtest & Coverage

Rolling-origin evaluation with 3-month step. Coverage = fraction of test outcomes falling inside the interval. Target: ≥ 90%. Winkler score rewards narrow + accurate intervals.
